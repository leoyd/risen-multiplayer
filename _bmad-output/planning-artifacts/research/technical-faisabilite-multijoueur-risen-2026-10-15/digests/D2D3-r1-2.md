## Leads worth chasing
- Check whether the 64-bit Script.dll still loads `bin/scripts/Script_*.dll` and what the `ScriptInit` signature is. This would be a clean, injector-free entry point. Inspect the local binaries for the "Script_" string and the ScriptInit lookup.
- WoP threads 886883 (RisenSDK) and 1061573 (sample project) are blocked by Anubis for bots. Open them manually for SDK headers (class layouts of gCSession/eCEntity, 32-bit) and use them as an RE reference for the 64-bit build.
- Check whether anyone (VaanaCZ, the Elusively Patched Unit author) ported their fixes to the 64-bit build. That would be proof that x64 script DLLs work.
- Check SteamDB (app 40300) for depots and DRM, to confirm there is no SteamStub on the 64-bit exe.
- Check whether Wine 11.6's load-order heuristic is in the current Proton (10.x/11.x) or Proton Experimental.
- Look in the DXVK issue tracker for D3D9 EndScene/Present hook reports. ReShade and MangoHud on D3D9 under DXVK are known-working analogues (unverified this run).
